What to skip

Half of getting good is knowing what not to do.

  • A 40-hour “Python from zero” courseIf you can already code: ship one small deployed service instead.
  • Three web frameworks before you ship onePick one. Put it on a public URL.
  • Paid prompt-engineering bootcampsThe free docs and your own evals teach more.
  • Reading every agent framework’s docsLearn the loop first. Frameworks are a thin layer on top.
  • Tutorials you follow without changing anythingRebuild it with your own data and see what breaks.

What this is not

  • Not a course aggregator. Every resource gets a verdict.
